Before you begin

Talk instead of typing: Tap the microphone beside any written question, speak, then tap Stop. Review and edit the transcript before exporting. Your browser may ask for microphone permission. Speech recognition is browser-dependent and may send audio to the browser provider for transcription; avoid identifying details. If unavailable, use your phone keyboard’s dictation microphone or type.

Participation is voluntary. You may skip any question or stop at any time. Do not include patient names, identifying patient information, coworker names, or hospital names. Responses may be summarized in aggregate. Direct quotations would require separate permission.

Pilot preview: This form is not connected to a response database. Selecting “Export my responses” downloads your answers onto your own device; it does not send them to the project team. A secure collection service and reviewed privacy/consent procedures are needed before public distribution.
